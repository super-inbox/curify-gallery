# 三视图 / three-view — example cases (2026-08-17)

Factory-facing three-view sheets: orthographic views plus a **code-drawn** spec
band (dimensions, material/CMF, proportion). Generated by
`curify-studio/dev/jayw/design-agent-v0/threeview/image_to_threeview.py`.

Why this matters: the 2026-08-16 roadmap puts 毛绒 and 潮玩/手办 at ⭐⭐ and both
bottleneck on the same artifact — 三视图. A factory can quote from a dimensioned
three-view; it cannot quote from a render.

**AI draws the views; code draws every number.** The prompt forbids the model
from rendering dimension lines or callouts, because a number on a drawing reads
as authoritative and the model's numbers are decorative.

## The three cases, and what they show together

| case | input | result |
|---|---|---|
| **haarland** | our own chibi turnaround sheet (front/side/back, one scale) | ✅ **the reference case** — `--band-only`, no model call at all |
| **dragon** | one photo of a metal dragon figurine | ✅ coherent front/side/top, labelled, consistent scale |
| **snake** | one photo of a coiled snake figurine | ❌ three *different* sculptures — loop topology differs per view |

### The lesson is about the INPUT, not the prompt

The snake failed because a single photo of a self-occluding glossy coil does not
*contain* the 3D form: which loop passes in front is ambiguous, so the model
re-invented the topology per view. Count the loops across the three panels — no
camera angle changes that. The dragon succeeded because its head, limbs and fins
pin the geometry across angles.

No amount of prompt tightening recovers information the photo does not hold. So:

- **already a coherent turnaround → `--band-only`.** No model, no quota, and the
  snake failure cannot occur by construction. This is the reliable path.
- **one photo → views.** Works on subjects with clear landmarks, fails silently
  on self-occluding ones. Needs a consistency gate before it is customer-facing;
  today nothing distinguishes a dragon-quality sheet from a snake-quality one
  without a human looking.

## Honesty rules baked into the sheets

- Every sheet footers **"not a tooling drawing — confirm tolerances with the factory."**
- The haarland band states its dimensions are **"a target spec for tooling, not
  measured from a sample"** — they are assumed, and the artifact says so rather
  than relying on a caption.
- ⚠️ The dragon and snake dimensions are **estimates read off a photo**, and both
  subjects are another supplier's booth products. Do not publish those two as
  marketing without measured dimensions and permission.

## Not produced here

No STL / STEP / .blend. That needs a mesh, a single photo does not determine
hidden geometry, and a wrong mesh costs a steel mould rather than a re-render.
Staged path: 三视图 → `.glb` for approval only → `.stl`/`.step` with wall
thickness, draft angle and a watertight check.
