# Ad-variant demo — 2026-09-10

Attachment for the `marketing_agency` voice (paid-media agencies).

## Why this artifact and not a portfolio

A media buyer's bottleneck is **volume**: the winning ad decays and the next test is
not built. Describing that costs a paragraph and proves nothing. One source asset
becoming nine usable creatives *is* the offer, rendered — it can be judged in about
two seconds without reading the email.

## Provenance

- Generated end-to-end from our own prompt (`gen_variants.py`, prompt inline).
- `00-source.png` is **synthetic**. No real product, no brand, no logo, and the label
  is deliberately blank — nothing here belongs to a client or a third party.
- The nine variants were made by feeding `00-source.png` BACK IN and changing only the
  setting. Two independently generated images would be two different bottles, which
  would prove nothing about consistency — the entire claim is that the product is
  locked while the scene varies.

## What it demonstrates

Same bottle, same dropper, same blank label, same camera angle, across nine settings:
white packshot, marble, bathroom, outdoor sun, dark luxe, flat-lay, gift, botanical,
pastel podium. `_one-to-nine.jpg` (1272x1118, 178KB) is the single attachable file.

## Still true

No delivered-outcome claim attaches to this. It shows what we can run, not what a
named client accepted.
