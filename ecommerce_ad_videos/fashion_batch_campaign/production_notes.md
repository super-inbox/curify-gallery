# Curify fashion batch ad

22-second vertical campaign demonstration, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps.

Creative: One product → a whole campaign. Studio product, model, lifestyle, detail, moving batch grid, inquiry CTA.

CTA: DM “BATCH” for a quote. Send store link and product count.

The supplied Curify poster was used as a visual/product reference. Three new scene images were made using the built-in image-generation tool, then animated using MiniMax-H3 through the CN API. This is an AI campaign demonstration, not footage of a customer project. Product geometry can vary between generated scenes.

Audio: original synthesized instrumental music bed; no third-party song or voiceover.

Image prompt set: each scene requests a full-bleed, photorealistic 9:16 image, preserving the reference ivory pebbled-leather bag, arched handle, cream strap and gold clasp; no text or watermarks. Scenes: warm travertine studio product photo; brunette adult model in cream tailoring on a sunlit limestone street; Mediterranean terrace product scene with sea, white architecture and linen.

## MiniMax motion prompts

### street

Luxury fashion editorial film. The adult woman makes a subtle graceful quarter turn toward the camera. A light breeze moves her brunette hair and ivory blazer. Camera slowly tracks closer, keeping the cream handbag fully visible near the center. Preserve the bag exact geometry, handle, strap, leather and gold clasp. Natural realistic motion, anatomically correct hands. Single continuous shot, no cuts, no text, no logos, no speech or music.

### product

A luxury handbag campaign film. Very slow controlled camera dolly toward the ivory handbag on travertine. A soft glint travels over the gold clasp. Keep the bag completely rigid and identical to the reference: preserve seams, silhouette, top handle, strap, pebbled texture and gold lock. Warm architectural sunlight. Single continuous shot. No text, no logos, no speech or music.

### terrace

Luxury product campaign film. Gentle lateral camera slider motion around the stationary ivory handbag on a Mediterranean terrace. Subtle breeze ripples the linen at the table edge, sea sparkles in soft background bokeh. Preserve handbag silhouette, top handle, strap, leather texture and gold clasp exactly. Keep entire bag visible. Natural warm sunlight. Single continuous shot. No text, logos, speech or music.
