## V14 · Still life (was S2) — make the material do the work

Attach: `02-still-life-page2-v3.png`

For a still-life brief, “premium” needs something more specific to work with.

Glass bends the background. Amber liquid changes the light. Suede absorbs it. A silver ring against cashmere brings a different kind of contrast.

This board brings together eight AI-generated concepts for a commercial studio: fragrance with liquid and bubbles, an amber glass macro, a balanced bottle-and-coupe composition, sculptural shoes, leather accessories and jewelry studies.

The effects have a job: make the material and silhouette easier to feel. That gives a client useful choices to discuss before a production direction is set.

These are original AI concept studies. The bottles, accessories and jewelry are imagined examples, not verified reproductions of a client's products.

Which direction would you explore first for your next brief: fluid and glass, sculptural accessories or tactile jewelry?
