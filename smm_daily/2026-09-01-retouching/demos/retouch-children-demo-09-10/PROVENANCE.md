## Provenance

- Generated end-to-end from our own prompt (`gen_retouch_sets.py`, prompt inline).
- `00-source.png` is **synthetic**. No real child appears in any frame, and nothing here derives
  from a client's material.
- The four variants were produced by feeding `00-source.png` BACK IN and changing only the
  backdrop. Two independently generated images would be two different children, which proves
  nothing about a locked subject — and locked subject is the entire claim.
- Standard clothed commercial school-portrait framing throughout, which is what this ICP sells.
