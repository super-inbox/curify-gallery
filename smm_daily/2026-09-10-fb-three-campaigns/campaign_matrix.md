## The mechanism, and why the earlier groups failed

We are no longer hunting people who already posted *"I need outsourcing"*. We enter groups where the
ICP is already dense and let a highly relevant demo make buyers raise their own hand.

`Product Photography`-type groups failed because **the group clustered by profession, so it gathered
supply**. The rule now:

> **The group taxonomy must match the BUYER taxonomy, not the service taxonomy.**

Souvenir video → cut by **place × tourism trade**. Ecommerce → cut by **product category**.
Retouching → cut by **place** (destination work) or **genre** (studio work).

⚠️ **The two families split by BUYER, not by subject.** Apparel appears in both only if you get
this wrong: a clothing BRAND that needs listing images is an ecommerce cell (B3, served by the
existing `ecommerce_workflow` videos); a STUDIO that shoots people and needs post is a retouching
cell (C3). Never pitch retouching to a brand or listing packs to a photographer.

# The experiment, and what it is actually measuring

**3 families × 5 niches × 5 groups = 75 placements, but only ~15 demos** — each demo posted into 3–5
tightly matched groups.

Do **not** automate posting. Manual, and one group per demo per ~3 days.

Log per placement:

```
date | family | niche | group | members | buyer type | demo | views | likes | comments | DMs | samples | paid
```

The output is not *"does Facebook work"*. It is **which `Offer × Niche × Group` cell works** — e.g.
`Bali photographer × retouching` strong, `generic ecommerce × AI images` zero. Then expand the
neighbourhood of a winning cell: `Bali → Phuket → Maldives → Santorini`, or
`jewelry → watches → accessories → handbags`.

Same denominator columns as email, RedNote and Instagram, in
`gtm_tools/outreach_denominator.csv`, so the channels stay comparable.

  `105108073643190` (E-commerce product photography, 5.2K) carries an **explicit ban rule** — read it
