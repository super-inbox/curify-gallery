## Production

Original adult models, completely flat reclining poses and locked overhead cameras. Template images guide garment motifs; the output is an AI fashion interpretation, not a documentary reconstruction. Ukrainian / Mexican / Uzbek looks retain their distinct named references; the atlas robe is interpreted as contemporary womenswear.

Nine controlled stills generated with imagegen. First/last-frame pairs rendered with MiniMax-Hailuo-02 (1080P, 6s each), two transitions per video. Foreground glowstick light sweeps or fan-blade occlusion drive the changes. The sari still was corrected for an extra hand before animation. No reference video footage or audio was reused.

Editing: two retimed transitions (brief introduction, accelerated foreground sweep and slowed final reveal), plus a 10-frame final-pose hold, total 12s; then the identical 3s Curify ending from the previous batch. Optical-flow interpolation smooths the final reveal. Final video is exactly 450 frames at 30fps. Music from the gallery music library, normalized to -16 LUFS with a fade-out. English / Chinese labels burned in. CTA: **Batch fashion try-on · curify-ai.com**.
