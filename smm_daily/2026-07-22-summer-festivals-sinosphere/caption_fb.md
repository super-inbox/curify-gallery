## FB post copy

☀️ Same season, three completely different ways to feel summer 🇯🇵🇰🇷🇨🇳

🇯🇵 **JAPAN — the matsuri (夏祭り).** Yukata and food stalls after dark (takoyaki, shaved ice, goldfish scooping), river *hanabi* fireworks lighting up the night, and Bon-Odori circle dances with portable shrines carried through the streets.

🇰🇷 **KOREA — mud and heat.** The Boryeong Mud Festival on the beach, and on the hottest "Boknal" days, hot samgyetang to beat heat *with* heat (이열치열). Then straight to Haeundae and the summer rock festivals.

🇨🇳 **CHINA — the night market (夜市).** Crayfish, sizzling skewers and cold beer at open-air stalls, families out "cooling off" (乘凉) with fans and chilled watermelon, and river-drifting escapes from the 三伏天 dog-days heat.

Same continent, one shared instinct: the best of summer happens *after dark*, once the heat finally breaks. Which one's your kind of summer? 👇
