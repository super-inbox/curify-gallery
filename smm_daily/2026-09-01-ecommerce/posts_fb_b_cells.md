# B6–B9 · Cells added 2026-09-16 from the demand mining

_Source: [`curify-gtm/docs/reddit-demand-mining-ecommerce-2026-09-15.md`](../../../curify-gtm/docs/reddit-demand-mining-ecommerce-2026-09-15.md)
— 836 posts, 7 lanes. **B1–B5 above are cut by product category. These four are cut by JOB**, because
that is what the corpus says the buyer actually asks about. Both cuts are valid and they stack: a
jewelry seller can get B1, an Amazon jewelry seller can get B6._

**Three findings from the mining that govern every cell below:**

1. ⭐ **This is a vendor-shopping market.** Across 836 posts, `agency` (46) and `photographer` (27)
   outnumber every purpose-built AI tool combined (14). They ask **"who do I hire"**, not "what do I
   buy". So these posts sell *someone doing the work*, never a tool — the opposite of how we'd pitch
   a photographer.
2. ⛔ **Never write "AI-generated" as a selling point.** Two lanes carry *"does not look AI
   generated"* as a stated buyer requirement, and one seller's customer asked *"are your photos real
   or AI?"* before buying. **Sell the constraint** (the product doesn't change), never the automation.
3. ⚠️ **"Nine variants" is the wrong frame in a paid-ads context** — see the B2 correction below.

## ⚠️ B2 CORRECTION — re-cut the beauty cell from nine backgrounds to nine arguments

**B2 above is the only cell the mining says is actively mis-framed, and it should be edited before
it runs again.** The highest-engagement post in r/FacebookAds (32 comments, author claims $10M+ Meta
spend) argues:

> *"meta doesnt reward volume. it rewards variance. **50 creatives that are the same ad with a
> different background is ONE creative to the system.** it reads content semantically now… what moves
> is 5 creatives making 5 genuinely different arguments."*

B2 currently sells *"One bottle. Nine ad-ready variants — white packshot, marble, bathroom…"* — one
product, nine backgrounds. To a media buyer, that is the thing the ranker collapses.

⚠️ **This is one practitioner's claim, not a measured fact.** But B2's audience is exactly the people
who upvoted it, so the safe move is to change the *frame*, not the asset:

> **Keep for beauty/skincare brand owners (B2 as written).** A brand needs catalogue coverage —
> nine settings is nine usable listing and social images, and the pitch stands.
>
> **Re-cut for media buyers and agencies (B7 below).** Lead with *the product survives*, not with
> the count. The nine-up grid proves consistency; it should never be captioned "nine ads."
