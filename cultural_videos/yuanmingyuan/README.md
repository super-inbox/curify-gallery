# 圆明园今昔 · Yuanmingyuan, Then and Now

[15-second bilingual master](yuanmingyuan-then-and-now-15s.mp4) · 1080 × 1920 · 24 fps · stereo.

Three five-second views move from photographs of the ruins to illustrative reconstructions. The cut uses one original score and bilingual titles. The ruin photographs are cropped and animated with camera moves and dissolves.

- Great Fountain / 大水法, 2025. Photograph by Air7538. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yuanmingyuan_Ruins_of_Dashuifa_202510.jpg) · [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)
- Haiyantang / 海晏堂, 2013. Photograph by 颐园新居. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Yuanmingyuan_Haiyantang_20130126.JPG) · [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
- Wanfang Anhe / 万方安和, 2013. Photograph by 颐园新居. [Source](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:万方安和遗址_20130318.JPG) · [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)
